    \caption[Runtime versus time-of-day]{Per-iteration elapsed time, each normalised
    to the median of its own configuration, workload, and tier cell (relative
    runtime, reference line at \num{1.0}), plotted against the start time-of-day in
    UTC and coloured by system family, with a per-system ordinary-least-squares
    trend line. Because the \num{30} passes started at different wall-clock times,
    each cell's timed iterations spread across the clock; the trend lines are flat
    (overall Spearman $\rho = -0.01$, and $\lvert\rho\rvert \le 0.15$ within every
    system), indicating that no time-of-day or noisy-neighbour effect contaminates
    the measured runtimes.}
