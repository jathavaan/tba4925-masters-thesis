\section{Thesis outline}
\label{introduction:thesis-outline}
This thesis proceeds as follows. Chapter~\ref{ch:background} introduces the technical background, covering cloud-native data architecture, spatial geometry and indexing, spatial data formats and storage models, and distributed spatial processing. Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology} sets out the research design, defining the workloads, the time, transfer, and cost metrics, the statistical analysis, and the threats to validity. Chapter~\ref{ch:system-architecture-and-implementation} describes the system that realizes the design, from infrastructure and data layer to compute configuration, orchestration, and measurement instrumentation. Chapter~\ref{ch:results} reports the measured results across the single-machine queries and the distributed join. Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion} interprets these results against the research questions, and Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusions} concludes and outlines directions for future work.
