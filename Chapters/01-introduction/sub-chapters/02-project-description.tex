\section{Problem statement and objectives}
\label{introduction:problem-statement-and-objectives}
Two settings make up the comparison. The first issues single-machine queries over one dataset. The second is a national-scale spatial join that sets a distributed engine against single-node engines on the same data.

Three research questions frame the study's objectives. Because they follow from gaps in the existing benchmarking literature, they are stated formally in Section~\ref{related-work:research-questions}, after the related work that motivates them in Chapter~\ref{ch:related-work}. The first compares the cloud-native combination of GeoParquet and DuckDB against the traditional alternatives PostGIS and Shapefile; the second extends that comparison to the horizontal scaling of Apache Sedona against the single-node engines on the national-scale join; and the third asks whether the best-performing configuration is consistent across query patterns and outcome dimensions.
