\section{Contributions}
\label{introduction:contributions}
% Academic rewrite
This thesis contributes a benchmark that places vector cloud-native formats and engines beside their traditional counterparts on identical Norwegian building data and the same cloud storage, addressing gaps that prior spatial benchmarks leave open. The first contribution, framed as \acrshort{rq}1, compares the cloud-native combination of GeoParquet and DuckDB against PostGIS and Shapefile across spatial intersection, nearest-neighbor search, and bounding-box filtering. The second, framed as \acrshort{rq}2, sets the distributed engine Apache Sedona against single-node PostGIS and DuckDB on a national-scale spatial join across small, medium, and large dataset tiers, a comparison no published benchmark has reported. The third reports network transfer jointly with run-time and operational cost, a dimension absent from almost all spatial benchmarks. Alongside these, the study makes a methodological contribution by reporting effect sizes and bootstrapped confidence intervals and by adopting a rank-based omnibus test with post-hoc correction, practices rarely found in geospatial benchmarking.
