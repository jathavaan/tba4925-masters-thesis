\section{Contributions}
\label{introduction:contributions}
Running on identical Norwegian building data in the same cloud storage, the benchmark places vector cloud-native formats and engines beside their traditional counterparts, addressing gaps that prior spatial benchmarks leave open. Four contributions follow. The first, \acrshort{rq}1, compares the cloud-native combination of GeoParquet and DuckDB against PostGIS and Shapefile across spatial intersection, nearest-neighbor search, and bounding-box filtering. The second, \acrshort{rq}2, sets the distributed engine Apache Sedona against single-node PostGIS and DuckDB on a national-scale spatial join across small, medium, and large dataset tiers, a comparison no published benchmark has reported. The third reports network transfer jointly with run-time and operational cost, a dimension absent from almost all spatial benchmarks. The fourth is methodological: it reports effect sizes and bootstrapped \acrlonglc{ci}s~(\acrshort{ci}) and adopts a rank-based omnibus test with post-hoc correction, practices rarely found in geospatial benchmarking.
